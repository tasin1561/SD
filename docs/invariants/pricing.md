**Pricing rules (FLAT — replaced the zone/slab engine 2026-08-01):**
1. **One flat delivery fee per seller, anywhere in India.** `pricing.flat_delivery_fee` (seeded 200) with `pricing.flat_delivery_fee_currency` (seeded **BDT**), both resolved through SET-1: a per-seller override beats the global default, and the override is the one that counts. No zones, no weight slabs, no surcharges, no rate-card lookup. `PricingEngineService` reads settings and the FX rate, nothing else.
2. **A returned parcel costs delivery + `pricing.flat_rto_fee`** (seeded 30, currency BDT). The RTO half is NOT predicted at order create — it is charged when the parcel is physically RECEIVED (`RtoFeeAccrualService.chargeOnReceive`, hooked into `RtoReceiptService.receive`). A courier scan saying a parcel is coming back is not the parcel coming back; a debit taken on one would need an adjusting entry.
3. **The delivery fee is ALSO swept at RTO receive if unpaid.** An order that returns never reaches DELIVERED, so on AT_DELIVERY fee timing the outbound leg would otherwise be free. Idempotent via `OrderChargesAccrualService.debitIfNeeded`'s own gate.
4. **`WalletEntryDirection.RTO_FEE` is its own direction** — never folded into ORDER_CHARGES, so "what did returns cost this month" is answerable from the ledger alone. Per WAL-1 it is a DEBIT and therefore ABSENT from `CREDIT_DIRECTIONS` on the API side and returns `false` from `isWalletCredit` on the UI side. It is also EXCLUDED from the ORDER_CHARGES sum, or the fee would be taken twice with the second charge invisible inside another total.
5. **GST on the flat fees is `pricing.flat_fee_gst_percent`, seeded 0** — today the fee is what the seller pays, full stop. GLOBAL only, never seller-overridable: a tax rate is set by law, not negotiated. The GST line is written even at zero, because the invoice reads it and an absent line says "we forgot" rather than "none was charged". Distinct from `pricing.gst_rate`, which is the fallback GST on the GOODS.
6. **A fee that resolves to 0 is FLAGGED (`NO_FLAT_DELIVERY_FEE`), and `OrderChargesService` refuses to persist it.** Recording ₹0 as a real price bills the seller nothing for a parcel that cost money to move, and nothing fails on its own — a zero is a valid Decimal, the tx commits, the UI renders "₹0.00". Under the old engine this was the COMMON case (an unlisted pincode → "DEFAULT" zone → no match); flat pricing removes that seam but the guard stays.
7. Charges are computed at order create (post-commit, best-effort) and persisted to `order_charges` with `computationContext`. **Historical accuracy: past orders show charges as persisted — never recompute.** The rate-card / zone-matrix / surcharge tables still exist and are no longer read by anything.

8. **PRC-8 (2026-09-20): a fee is AGREED in a currency and CHARGED in rupees.** Every flat fee carries an amount key and a `_currency` partner (`FEE_CURRENCY_KEY` on the engine is the ONE map of which is which — the attention sweep derives its list from it rather than restating the keys), and BOTH are seller-overridable, so a negotiated fee is "৳250" or "₹180" as one decision rather than two rows that can disagree. A non-INR fee is converted at **the moment the charge is taken** through the SAME `inrRateAt` / `toInr` helpers the P&L and inbound freight use (TRE-6), so the rupees a seller is billed and the figure the report derives cannot diverge. **Delivery converts at order create; the RTO fee converts at RTO RECEIVE**, which is weeks later and legitimately a different rate — the two legs of one returned order may carry two, and each charge records its own. The charge's `computationContext.flatFee` stamps `agreedAmount`, `agreedCurrency`, `fxRate`, `fxRatePair`, `fxRateSource` and `fxRateRecordedAt`, because "why was I charged ₹162.60?" is unanswerable a month later otherwise. **`ResolvedFee.amount` was RENAMED to `agreedAmount`** on purpose: it was rupees for the whole life of the engine, and a caller that kept treating it as rupees would bill ৳200 as ₹200 and typecheck clean — the rename made every such call site fail to compile (it caught three). **An unpriceable fee is never a zero**: no FX rate is `NO_FX_RATE_FOR_FEE` and a currency setting holding a non-currency is `BAD_FEE_CURRENCY`, both flagged on their own terms so whoever reads them goes to the FX screen rather than the pricing screen. A return whose fee cannot be priced is RECEIVED normally and raises HIGH MONEY `return-fee-unpriced:<orderId>` — refusing a parcel already on the bench over an exchange rate is the worse failure. **The rate's own staleness is now a money problem, not a display one**: nothing fetches rates automatically (M16 deferred that), so `OrderAttentionService.checkStaleFeeFxRate` raises MEDIUM `fee-fx-rate-stale` when a currency in use has a rate older than 7 days, and clears itself. **The seeded defaults became ৳200 and ৳30 on 2026-09-20 (owner)** — about ₹162.60 and ₹24.39 at the rate on record, an intended price change and not an accident of conversion. The key names dropped their `_inr` suffix in the same change: a name asserting INR over a taka amount is exactly the kind of lie this schema has been bitten by before.
