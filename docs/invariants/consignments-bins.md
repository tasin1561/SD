**Two-leg consignment rules (CNS-1 through CNS-6, 2026-08-19 — NON-NEGOTIABLE). Design: `docs/consignment-two-leg.md`.**

1. **CNS-1: in-transit is a PLACE, not a gap.** Goods leave Dhaka on the 3rd and reach Bangalore on the 12th; for nine days they are in neither building, and booking them to either makes that building's cycle count find them missing. `BinType.TRANSIT` lives in the **destination** warehouse and is in `NON_PICKABLE_BIN_TYPES` — the constant `StockAvailabilityService` and `StockPickAllocationService` already share by construction (BIN-2), so in-transit stock is unsellable in the availability sum AND in the allocator through one line. `PICKABLE_BIN_TYPES` is now DERIVED as the complement; never re-list it. `TRANSIT` is deliberately absent from the bin CREATOR's type list — the other non-pickable types are places inside a building and this one is the absence of one, so a hand-made transit shelf is a category error; the dispatch provisions it like FLOOR.

2. **CNS-2: `Warehouse.fulfilsOrders` has exactly ONE reader** (`WarehouseResolverService`), the same discipline that put bin tracking behind `BinPolicyService`. Five call sites each deciding what "an Indian warehouse" means is how they come to disagree. `ops.bd_intake_warehouse_id` is seeded EMPTY on purpose and the resolver re-checks the flag on every read: a VIA_BD declaration is refused (`BD_WAREHOUSE_NOT_CONFIGURED`) rather than quietly routed to India, and an intake warehouse that still fulfils orders is refused too (`BD_WAREHOUSE_FULFILS_ORDERS`).

3. **CNS-3: a variance is a NUMBER, never a blocking state — and it moves in BOTH directions.** `GoodsReceiptService.complete` no longer routes to `DISCREPANCY`; it records `hasDiscrepancies` + notes and completes. The blocking gate earned its keep only where the held stock would become sellable, and nothing in Bangladesh is sellable from Bangladesh, so it stranded consignments waiting on an email. At the Indian end "India is final" means the count decides; a shortfall against the BD count is OURS to chase with the forwarder, and holding the seller's stock unsellable while we do that is backwards. **`resolveDiscrepancy` and its endpoint are DELETED — do not reintroduce a blocking count.** A count may be higher than what preceded it; `IN_TRANSIT_SURPLUS` is as normal as `IN_TRANSIT_LOSS`. **Since 2026-09-13 (TKT-3) a short or damaged count ALSO opens a `RECEIPT_SHORTFALL` ticket naming the leg, and a surplus sends a notice — both after the count commits, neither blocking anything.**

4. **CNS-4: an arrival MOVES stock, it never creates it.** `TransitArrivalService` fires for any leg with `dispatchedAt !== null`: `moved = min(received, in transit)` as a paired TRANSFER out of TRANSIT, the shortfall as `ADJUSTMENT_DECREASE`/`IN_TRANSIT_LOSS` out of the transit bin, the surplus as `ADJUSTMENT_INCREASE`/`IN_TRANSIT_SURPLUS` onto the shelf. Posting `RECEIVING` here would DOUBLE the goods and the copy would be the sellable one. The batch travels as a deterministic child (`<parent>-IN`) inheriting expiry, cost and lineage — the R6b pattern; a generic "August air shipment" batch would make six-month-old stock look as fresh as today's. The child's `receivingNoteId` points at the INDIA leg, not the Bangladesh parent's receipt, because the freight bill is amortised over units that land and the attribution walk must arrive at the leg carrying it.

5. **CNS-5: ONE labelling station per consignment, locked on first print.** `labellingSite` is BD | IN | NONE, chosen by ops (never on the seller's form), free to change until `labelsPrintedAt` is stamped — by a guarded `updateMany`, never a read-then-write. A consignment half-labelled in two countries cannot be told apart without opening every carton. Labelled in BD is the strongest version: units exist before the goods reach India, the dispatch MOVES them, and the arrival becomes a scan, so a shortfall is a **named list of missing serials**. The ONE place "nothing blocks" cannot hold is a strict SURPLUS at arrival — units with no serial cannot be picked (UNIT-2) — so they are labelled where they surfaced; the one-station rule is about where the WORK happens, not a ban on ever printing elsewhere.

6. **CNS-6: the cancel window CLOSES at dispatch, and cancelling REMOVES stock.** Abandoned goods go back to the seller as `ADJUSTMENT_DECREASE` with `RETURNED_TO_SELLER` (and the matching `StockUnitStatus`) — deliberately NOT a write-off: nothing was destroyed, somebody has them, and "what did we send back" is a different question from "what did we lose". Refusing after dispatch is load-bearing, not a preference: it makes unreachable the three states that have no answer — who eats freight already spent, where TRANSIT stock returns to, and what happens when a parcel lands against a consignment that no longer exists. **`consignments.status` is DERIVED by `ConsignmentStatusService` and never hand-set**; `consignment_events` is APPEND-ONLY. The freight bill is keyed on the GOODS RECEIPT (one LIVE bill per receipt — `inbound_freight_one_live_bill_per_receipt`, partial so a withdrawn bill can be replaced, FRT-7), NOT the consignment (corrected 2026-09-12): a consignment can land in more than one shipment, and each arrival is billed on its own receipt — production's CN-2026-08-000003 carries two bills (21 Aug and 6 Sep receipts), which is correct, not a double charge. `consignment_id` is a grouping column only. Each bill is split over its receipt's counted lines, and only a VIA_BD consignment is billable — enforced (`FREIGHT_NOT_BILLABLE`), not merely practised.

**Bin-tracking rules (BIN-1 through BIN-4, 2026-08-01 — NON-NEGOTIABLE):**

1. **BIN-1: "no bins" is not representable, so OFF is a real bin.** `stock_levels.bin_id` is NOT NULL and the unique key includes it. `warehouses.binTrackingEnabled` (per-warehouse, default false) decides whether the system ASKS for a location; when off, putaway resolves to that warehouse's `FLOOR` bin. **Off means a bin is not REQUIRED, not that one is refused** — an explicitly supplied real bin is still honoured, because it is a true fact either way and having it recorded is pure upside when tracking is switched on. `BinPolicyService` is the ONE reader of the flag; five call sites each testing it themselves is how they come to disagree. Every warehouse auto-gets a `MAIN` zone + `FLOOR` bin at creation — before that, creating a warehouse left you somewhere that could not receive stock and no screen to fix it, which is how production reached one warehouse and zero bins.

2. **BIN-2: INV-3 counts only what a picker can reach.** `qtyAvailable = SUM(qtyOnHand WHERE bin is pickable) − SUM(active reservations)`. `StockAvailabilityService` and `StockPickAllocationService` both derive that predicate from the single exported `NON_PICKABLE_BIN_TYPES` (RTO_HOLD / DAMAGED / QUARANTINE) in `bin-policy.service.ts`, and a test asserts they SHARE it rather than matching by coincidence. **This was a live bug**: availability summed every row while the allocator refused three bin types, so a return restocked into RTO_HOLD counted as sellable, an order confirmed and reserved against it, and the allocator then shortfalled — WMS-4 routing it to manual placement. The order did not fail where it was placed; it failed on the warehouse floor.

3. **BIN-3: a return is received, not shelved.** RTO restock lands in `RTO_HOLD` (falling back to the picked bin only when the warehouse has no hold bin) — at finalize the carton is on the returns bench, and booking it onto the shelf it was picked from claims a putaway nobody performed. `RtoPutawayService` is the person walking it somewhere and saying where; only then does BIN-2 make it sellable. It suggests `shipment_items.pickedBinId` when that bin is in the RECEIVING warehouse and still pickable, else the busiest pickable bin holding that SKU there. Refuses a non-pickable destination. **SUPERSEDED for returns booked at receive by WMS-8e (2026-09-14):** the returns hold now holds what came back and has NOT been decided (booked at receive); "Put back in stock" moves the unit straight to a sellable bin at finalize (FLOOR, or its old shelf when tracking is on), so there is no putaway step for it. Putaway remains only for units a pre-WMS-8e finalize left in hold, and never offers an undecided return.

4. **BIN-4: the toggle moves nothing; the collapse is separate and destructive.** Turning tracking on does not relocate FLOOR stock (it drains as it is picked); turning it off does not collapse existing bins. **Collapsing** — merging every bin into FLOOR — is `BinCollapseService`, SUPER_ADMIN only, behind a ≥30-char reason, the warehouse code typed exactly, and a six-digit code emailed to the actor (claimed via guarded `updateMany`, never read-then-write). **Snapshot FIRST, merge SECOND** (visible-vs-silent): a crash between leaves a complete backup and an untouched warehouse. The merge CANNOT be `UPDATE stock_levels SET bin_id = floor` — the unique key includes bin, so two bins holding the same variant+batch violate it; each row moves as a paired TRANSFER_OUT/TRANSFER_IN through `StockMutationService` (INV-1) and quantities accumulate. Hold/damaged/quarantine bins are EXCLUDED from a collapse (they are about not selling stock, not finding it). Restore is best-effort per line with a report — stock sells in between, so it is a head start, not a rewind. Backups expire on `ops.bin_snapshot_retention_months` (3). **Bin codes are COMPOSED from aisle+rack+shelf, never typed** — free text is how one shelf becomes `A-01-03`, `A-1-3` and `a01-03`.

24. Honor CNS-1 through CNS-6 (two-leg consignments — `docs/consignment-two-leg.md`). In-transit stock lives in a `TRANSIT` bin in the DESTINATION warehouse and is non-pickable through the one shared constant (CNS-1); `PICKABLE_BIN_TYPES` is DERIVED — never re-list it, and never offer `TRANSIT` in the bin creator. `Warehouse.fulfilsOrders` has ONE reader (CNS-2) — never test `countryCode` at a call site to decide whether a warehouse can ship. A count variance is a recorded NUMBER in BOTH directions, never a blocking state (CNS-3) — do not reintroduce `resolveDiscrepancy` or any gate that withholds stock over a count. A leg with `dispatchedAt` set MOVES stock out of TRANSIT and must never post `RECEIVING` (CNS-4). The labelling station is ONE place per consignment, locked by a guarded `updateMany` on first print (CNS-5). Cancel is refused after dispatch — and refused while a LIVE freight bill sits on the consignment (`CONSIGNMENT_FREIGHT_BILLED`, FRT-7: withdraw the bill first, or the seller keeps a debit for a shipment that never happened) — and removes stock as `RETURNED_TO_SELLER`, not a write-off (CNS-6); `consignments.status` is DERIVED by `ConsignmentStatusService` and `consignment_events` is append-only; the freight bill is keyed on the goods receipt (one per arrival, several per consignment) and only VIA_BD is billable.
